# Showcase Pages

## Responsibility

A Showcase page presents attributable work created by customers, community members, or users of a product so visitors can discover examples, judge what is possible, and follow a valid path to the work, creator, template, or product.

## Page Contract

Define eligible work, source and ownership, creator attribution, publication permission, curation or ranking method, categories, detail destination, submission path, moderation state, removal process, and the page's relationship to templates, case studies, and community profiles.

## Sections

- **Core:** page purpose, discoverable collection of work, creator attribution, and item destination.
- **Common:** categories, filters, featured work, product or format context, related examples.
- **Conditional:** submission invitation, moderation guidance, creator profile, template reuse, social sharing, report or removal action.

## Boundaries

A Showcase demonstrates user-created output. A Case Study explains an attributable customer problem and outcome; a Portfolio represents one creator or studio; a Template offers a reusable starting artifact. Do not convert unverified customer logos, scraped work, or internal concepts into implied endorsements.

## Components And Verification

Grid, Masonry, Card, Carousel, Task Input, Author Bio, Tabs, Modal, Social Share, and CTA may apply. Verify ownership, consent, attribution links, media rights, moderation, duplicate submissions, sensitive content, broken destinations, filters, pagination, responsive media, performance, accessibility, metadata, and removal requests.
