# Resource Pages

## Shared Responsibility

Resource pages help users discover, evaluate, and obtain maintained educational or practical assets. Content strategy decides what resources exist; Pagina builds their website surfaces.

## Modes

- **Hub:** orient across resource types and major themes.
- **Category:** browse one resource type or topic with filters, pagination, and useful category context.
- **Detail:** explain one asset, show a preview, attribution, update status, use instructions, and related resources.
- **Gated:** collect only justified information, record consent, and deliver the promised asset through a real success path.

## Components And Verification

Navigation, Grid, List, Card, Tabs, Newsletter, CTA, Modal, and Trust Signals may apply. Verify taxonomy, deduplication, empty and large collections, access and download links, asset version, form delivery, consent, repeat requests, localization, metadata, indexing, and expired or withdrawn resources.
