# About Page

## Responsibility

An About page establishes who operates the product or publication, why it exists, why its claims deserve trust, and how responsibility is handled. It is not a generic company-history montage or a replacement for author, careers, legal, or contact pages.

## Sections

- **Core:** operator identity, purpose, operating model, credibility, responsibility.
- **Common:** team or founder, methodology, independence or funding, corrections, contact path.
- **Conditional:** milestones, press, careers, detailed governance, data methodology.

Use confirmed names, roles, metrics, ownership, funding, and policies. Single-person products should not invent a team voice. Publications and research products need stronger methodology and correction signals; SaaS products usually emphasize company, product purpose, and trust.

## Components And Verification

Author Bio, Trust Signals, Testimonials, Timeline-like Lists, CTA Section, and Footer may support the page. Verify factual attribution, links to people and policies, structured identity data where used, localization, mobile hierarchy, and that every credibility claim is supportable.
