# Service And Solution Pages

## Shared Responsibility

Service and solution pages connect a defined customer situation to a credible path toward an outcome. They must make scope, fit, evidence, and the next commercial step concrete.

## Modes

- **Service hub:** organize the services a company actually delivers and help visitors select the relevant engagement.
- **Service detail:** state audience, problem, scope, deliverables, process, dependencies, exclusions, evidence, commercial model, and next step.
- **Solution hub:** organize outcome-led offers by industry, team, company size, or another defensible segment.
- **Solution detail:** connect segment-specific constraints and outcomes to relevant product capabilities, services, proof, and adoption path.

## Boundaries

Services describe work delivered for or with a customer. Solutions package products and services around an outcome or segment. Use cases describe a concrete scenario and may support a Solution page without becoming a synonym for it.

## Components And Verification

Hero, Navigation, Grid, Card, How-to, Comparison Table, Testimonials, Trust Signals, FAQ, Contact path, and CTA may apply. Verify scope, qualifications, geographic availability, process ownership, timelines, unsupported outcome claims, lead routing, consent, proof attribution, and links to related use cases and case studies.
