# Landing Page

## Responsibility

A landing page converts a defined audience arriving with a known expectation toward one primary outcome. It is narrower than a homepage and must preserve message continuity from its traffic source.

## Page Contract

Identify audience, source, promise, required proof, offer, primary action, success destination, and measurement. Do not invent urgency, scarcity, testimonials, results, or product capabilities.

## Sections

- **Core:** promise and action; problem or intent match; mechanism or offer; proof.
- **Common:** benefits, objections, use case, demonstration, trust.
- **Conditional:** comparison, calculator or tool input, lead form, FAQ, qualification, secondary path.

The page may be short or long. Length follows decision complexity, not a fixed section count.

## Product Adaptation

SaaS pages usually connect pain, workflow, product evidence, and signup or demo. Tool pages may make the real task input primary. Ecommerce campaign pages connect offer, products, fulfillment, and purchase. Professional services require fit, process, evidence, and contact qualification.

## Components

Hero and CTA Section are common candidates; trust, testimonials, FAQ, comparison, task input, and newsletter are conditional. Avoid carousels when they hide proof or competing messages.

## Verify

Check source-to-page message match, one clear primary action, form and success states, mobile reading order, evidence quality, metadata and campaign parameters, loading performance, analytics, and absence of unsupported claims.
