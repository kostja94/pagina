# Campaign Pages

## Shared Responsibility

Campaign pages support a time-bound promotion, event, launch, contest, waitlist, or offer with a defined audience, action, state lifecycle, and success path.

## Modes

- **Promotion:** explain a time-bound offer, eligibility, value, exclusions, redemption, and expiration.
- **Contest:** explain prize, eligibility, entry method, official rules, selection, timing, and disclosure.
- **Event:** explain schedule, venue or access, speakers, capacity, registration, attendance, and post-event state.
- **Waitlist:** set accurate expectations, collect only necessary information, and confirm what happens next.

## Lifecycle

Design scheduled, active, ended, unavailable, capacity reached, and archived states. Urgency must come from verified timing or inventory, never fabricated countdowns or participant counts.

## Components And Verification

Hero, Announcement Banner, Task Input or Form, How-to, FAQ, Trust Signals, Modal, Social Share, and CTA Section may apply. Verify time zones, eligibility, consent, official rules, capacity, confirmation, calendar or access delivery, abuse controls, analytics, expired routes, redirects, localization, and regional restrictions.
