# Page Quality Gates

## Product And Content

- The page has one clear job and a recognizable primary path.
- Claims, prices, dates, evidence, legal text, and capabilities come from reliable project sources.
- The page answers the target audience's real decision or task without generic filler.
- Optional sections exist because content and user needs justify them.

## Structure And Composition

- Information hierarchy follows the user's recognition, evaluation, action, and verification needs.
- Sections remain visually distinct without becoming a stack of decorative cards.
- Existing components and design tokens are reused where appropriate.
- Visible sections and technical component boundaries are chosen independently.

## Interaction And States

- Navigation, controls, forms, loading, empty, error, success, and permission states behave coherently.
- Keyboard order, focus, labels, headings, contrast, motion, and assistive semantics are usable.
- Long content, translations, missing assets, narrow screens, and touch input do not break the layout.

## Web And Delivery

- The route, canonical identity, metadata, structured data, indexing behavior, and social preview match the page type.
- Performance-sensitive media and scripts are justified, sized, and loaded appropriately.
- Analytics and conversion events are added only through the project's established system.
- The page is verified in its real product context, not only as an isolated preview.
